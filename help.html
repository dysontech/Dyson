Help centre

Quick answers. More in the app under Settings › Help & Support.

How do I start a chat?

Open Chats, tap the pencil or + button, search for a person or pick a contact.

Why can’t I make calls?

Allow camera and microphone access, use HTTPS, and make sure the other person has Dyson open. On strict mobile networks a TURN server may be needed.

How do voice notes work?

In a chat, tap the microphone, speak, then tap send. Tap the bin to cancel.

What is Dyson Vault?

A private file space. Files are encrypted in your browser with your passphrase before upload. Keep the passphrase safe — it cannot be recovered.

How do I go live?

Open Dyson Live, tap Go live, give it a title. Followers are notified. Works best for small audiences.

How do I change language or theme?

Settings › Language and Settings › Appearance.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in page choose “Forgot password?” and follow the emailed link.
