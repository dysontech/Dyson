Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy describes how this DYSON app handles data. Replace the bracketed details and have it reviewed before public launch.

What we store

  • Account: email (or phone-based login id), name, username, photo, about/bio.
  • Messages, reactions, status updates, posts, events, business listings, tasks and notes you create.
  • Files you send (chat media) and Vault files (stored encrypted; the server cannot read them).
  • Call history (who, when, how long). Call audio and video are not recorded or stored.
  • Presence (online / last seen), shown according to your privacy settings.

Where it lives

Data is stored with Supabase (database, authentication, file storage and realtime). Access rules in the database restrict each row to the people who should see it.

Third-party services the app may contact

  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr (fonts and the Supabase script).
  • STUN (and optionally your TURN) servers to connect calls.
  • MyMemory (text you ask the assistant to translate) and Wikipedia (lookups you ask for).
  • Google, if you choose “Continue with Google”.

Your controls

  • Settings › Privacy: last seen, online status, read receipts, who can message you, discoverability, blocked users.
  • Settings › Account: download your data, sign out everywhere, delete your account.
  • Delete a message for everyone from the message menu.

Retention

Status updates disappear after 24 hours. Deleting your account removes your profile and content.
